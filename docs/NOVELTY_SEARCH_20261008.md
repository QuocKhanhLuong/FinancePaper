## Scope and evaluation criteria, fixed before selecting a candidate

Question: what new, falsifiable knowledge or computational capability is needed
to evaluate/use explanations when some input information is missing?

- Existing predictor and historical Taiwan/Polish endpoints remain frozen.
  Inspected cohorts cannot select a model, threshold or new success endpoint.
- Human relevance/benefit and expert approval remain NOT RUN. Mathematical
  fixtures and AI review are not human judgments or expert peer review.
- A candidate needs a precise object/assumptions, a distinction from the closest
  primary-source method, and an exact falsifier or strong matched comparator.
- Generic uncertainty, a different head, VOI, DRO, selective prediction,
  conformal calibration, AIPW, a larger benchmark or new terminology do not
  establish originality. Reject a candidate that reduces to such a baseline.
- Feasibility is a gate, not evidence of novelty. Search absence is not proof
  that a result has never appeared. Any surviving originality claim is bounded
  by the documented sources and remains subject to external review.
- Evaluation is qualitative: nontriviality, distinctness from prior work,
  decision relevance, falsifiability, and executable feasibility. No weighted
  score is allowed to override a known equivalence or failed assumption.

## Independent generation

The scientific-brainstorming skill requests private initial generation followed
by an adversarial review from someone who did not originate the idea. Three AI
agents were assigned distinct theory/problem/algorithm perspectives with the
same constraints, without seeing each other's proposals. Their complete reports
are inputs to review, not votes or human validation. The coordinator independently
recorded the following candidate before targeted literature search.

**C0 — exact completion moments of tree attributions (IDEA, not a finding).**
For a fixed tree ensemble, independent reference features and independent
conditional completion features, derive exact first and joint second moments
of initially observed-feature interventional Shapley contributions using tree
leaf regions, rather than repeated complete-input explanation calls. Preserve
repeated-split interval intersections. A potential mechanism is the multilinear
leaf representation and a two-variable polynomial integral for products of
Shapley contributions. This is only potentially useful if existing moment/
probabilistic-database algorithms do not already provide the same computation.

Predictions to falsify: exact enumeration of finite completions matches the
calculation; shared-feature interval intersections are necessary for covariance;
computational cost does not depend on the number of possible completions.
Nearest-work threats: expected SHAP over missing features, uncertainty-aware SHAP,
probabilistic SHAP, covariance of Shapley values and polynomial tree explainers.
Independent completion is a strong assumption; moments alone do not identify
reason-failure probability or validate a disclosure decision.
