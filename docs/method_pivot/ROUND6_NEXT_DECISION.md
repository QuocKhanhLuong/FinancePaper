# Round 6 — close generic contrast projection as a method direction

Decision and bounded primary-source refresh: **2026-10-06**.
**NO-GO for further method implementation based on ordinary conditioning,
linear contrasts and identical-term reuse alone.** Keep the reusable numerical
audit/control. Do not rename it, train another predictor, adjust the target or
increase the customer sample to seek a more attractive result.

## What the executed step established

The [registered structural audit](ROUND6_STRUCTURAL_PROTOCOL.md), with its
[documented preflight amendment](ROUND6_PREFLIGHT_INCIDENT.md), ran on 32 reused
development customers across four missing conditions. Generic conditioning
substantially reduces the compiled representation; contrasts add no union
contraction and no practical one-query advantage over the same specialized
full-group evaluator. [All measured values and limitations](ROUND6_STRUCTURAL_RESULTS.md)
are preserved.

The audit also exposed a flaw in its own headroom screen: candidate signs plus
all competitor contrasts are an injective transformation of available group
coefficients. Their union cannot disappear through linear projection. This is
a useful algebraic correction, **not** proof that all direct-event inference is
intractable or useless. Sparse scalar contrasts, early Boolean decisions or
bound propagation can behave differently; this round neither develops nor
benchmarks those mechanisms. No new method has been empirically defeated if it
was never implemented.

**Strongest rejection:** after conditioning the same expression and completion
law, a generic evaluator obtains the exact same values and reuse opportunities.
The proposed contrast map preserves all available-group information. A faster
comparison against only unconditioned full SHAP would attribute ordinary
specialization gains to a nonexistent new method.

**Defensible difference:** no new necessary mechanism is currently derived.
There is a useful domain-specific frozen evaluation target, reproducible
attribution compilation controls and a corrected limitation of the search, but
these support application/evaluation evidence. They do not establish a new
inference algorithm, risk guarantee, causal explanation or accuracy gain.

## Scope of the decision

This closes **this generic contrast-inference branch**, not every learning
approach under missing observations. Historical learned-selector, Stable-Core,
one-field acquisition and conformal-specification negatives retain their exact
original scopes. Historical Taiwan/Polish financial results were not updated.
The observed-information/hidden-information problem remains real; its existence
does not make every proposed solution novel.

No 3-seed method matrix, full-scale dataset run or architecture is justified by
this round. Returning narrower uncertainty or constant reasons is not a fallback
success criterion. The supported paper remains an evaluation/policy study;
a method-paper claim is **not yet supported**, and no venue acceptance assessment
is added.
